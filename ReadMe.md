# Global AI Content Impact Dataset

## Overview
This dataset provides insights into AI adoption and its impact across various industries and countries. It includes metrics such as AI adoption rates, AI-generated content volume, job loss percentages, revenue increases, and more.

## Dataset Details
- **Industries Covered**: Media, Retail, Healthcare, Automotive, Gaming, etc.
- **Countries Included**: South Korea, USA, India, China, France, and more.
- **Time Period**: 2020–2025
- **Key Metrics**:
  - AI Adoption Rate (%)
  - AI-Generated Content Volume (TBs per year)
  - Job Loss Due to AI (%)
  - Revenue Increase Due to AI (%)
  - Human-AI Collaboration Rate (%)
  - Top AI Tools Used
  - Regulation Status
  - Consumer Trust in AI (%)
  - Market Share of AI Companies (%)

## Usage
This dataset can be used for:
- Analyzing trends in AI adoption and its impact.
- Building predictive models for AI-driven business strategies.
- Researching the socio-economic effects of AI in various industries.
